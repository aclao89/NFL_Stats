THE MODEL NOTES

Know what the number means.

This app combines historical NFL data with editable scenarios. Historical game projections use trained regressions with a chronological backtest; player projections fit a selected recorded-game sample. There is no live injury, starting-role, weather, or betting-odds feed.

Game outcomes

Expected margin = home projected points minus away projected points. Expected total = their sum. A normal distribution with the fitted or manually specified margin or total SD estimates the selected event. These are separate approximations, not a joint score simulation. Integer results use a 0.5-point continuity correction. Ties and pushes are shown separately; a favorite at −3.5 must win by at least 4.

Player yardage

A normal approximation uses your projected mean and game-to-game SD. The adjustment changes the mean only. Past results can supply a simple mean and sample SD. The model can assign probability to negative yards and does not account for skew, usage changes, opponent strength, or specific football scoring patterns.

Count stats and touchdowns

A Poisson model uses your expected count per game. Its mean and variance are equal. For at least one touchdown, probability = 1 − exp(−rate). Actual football counts may have different variance, clustering, and changing opportunities. Historical scoring touchdowns count rushing plus receiving touchdowns only. Passing, return, defensive, and fumble-recovery touchdowns are excluded. These are separate from the passing-touchdown stat.

Historical check

Observed hit rate = matching results divided by all entered games. The 95% Wilson interval describes uncertainty in that historical hit rate under an independent, constant-rate binomial assumption. It is not a confidence interval for the prediction or a backtest. Every entered game is equally weighted. Small or non-comparable samples can be misleading.

Price comparison

The quoted price determines break-even probability. Model edge compares it with model win probability conditional on no push. Expected net return per $100 = win probability × net win payout minus loss probability × $100. Those calculations inherit all model assumptions.

Data and privacy

Historical mode loads nflverse-derived snapshots and displays the coverage of each dataset. Check for updates retrieves the latest published GitHub snapshot, with an included snapshot as fallback. Manual examples remain illustrative. Saved scenarios stay in browser storage and can be exported.
